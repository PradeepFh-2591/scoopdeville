Keepin' it Philly!

Scoop DeVille has been keepin' it Philly since 1989. Pick your ice cream, pick your mix-ins, and we blend it fresh for you, one cup at a time. Every bite gets every flavor.

Not sure where to start? Try one of our Signature Blends, a milkshake, a sundae or a Fruit Whip. We have vegan and dairy-free options too.

Find us in Midtown and on South Street in Philadelphia, in Haddon Township, NJ, and on the North Wildwood boardwalk in season.
