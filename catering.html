Ice Cream Catering in Philadelphia & South Jersey

Scoop DeVille has been scooping in Philly since 1989 — and we bring the shop to you. Office parties, weddings, school events, tenant appreciation days: if there's a crowd, we'll set up a sundae bar they'll talk about for weeks.

What we cater

Sundae bars

Our ice cream, your choice of toppings, cups, spoons and napkins included. Small (5 oz), medium (8 oz) and large (11 oz) servings. Served two ways: pre-portioned cups (easy and quick) or scooped to order ("white glove").

Signature Blends

Available as a specialty request. We make them on the spot with our drill machines, just like in the stores. We only do this occasionally and it's priced as a premium, so ask us about it.

Shakes, edible cookie dough, and açaí bowls

Available as add-ons or standalone stations.

Custom ice cream cakes

Made in our shops for pickup or delivery to your event. (See our Cakes page.)
