Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

Scoop DeVille ("we," "us") runs ice cream shops in Philadelphia and New Jersey, along with catering, cakes, gifting and wholesale. This policy explains what information we collect through our website, online ordering and ads, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us, such as your name, email, phone number and company, when you request a catering quote, fill out a form (including forms inside our Google ads), place an order, or email or text us.
  • Event and order details, such as your event date, headcount, location and order items.
  • Payment information, which is processed by our payment providers. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Basic website and ad data, such as pages visited, the ad you clicked and device type, collected through cookies and tools like Google Ads and Google Analytics.

How we use it

  • To reply to your request, send quotes, and plan and deliver your order or event.
  • To process payments and send invoices and receipts.
  • To send occasional news and offers if you have signed up. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email.
  • To understand which ads and pages work, so we can improve our website and advertising.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the business, such as our online ordering, payment, email and advertising providers, and only as needed to provide their services. We may also share information if required by law.

Cookies and ads

Our website and ads use cookies to measure results and show relevant ads. You can control cookies in your browser settings and manage Google ad personalization at adssettings.google.com.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required for our business and tax records.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your contact information, or to stop sending you marketing emails, by contacting us below.

Children

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.
